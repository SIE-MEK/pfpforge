The anime PFP styles blowing up right now

Updated October 11, 2026 · 7 min read

Anime PFP Ideas 2026

Anime profile pictures have been a staple of the internet for twenty years, but the 2026 version is different. Instead of cropping a screenshot of your favourite character, people are turning their own face into anime art. It's personal, it's recognisable, and it doesn't get you mistaken for a fan account.

Here are the anime PFP styles trending on TikTok, Discord and X right now, and how to get each look from a normal selfie.

Why anime PFPs work so well

Anime art is built to read at small sizes. Bold outlines, flat colour and oversized eyes survive being shrunk to a tiny circle much better than a detailed photo does. That's why anime avatars dominate Discord member lists and comment sections: they're legible.

1. Soft shoujo pastels

Inspired by romance and slice-of-life series: sparkly eyes, rosy cheeks and a pastel palette of pink, lilac and baby blue. This is the most popular anime PFP style on TikTok this year.

Get the look: use a bright, evenly lit selfie with a gentle smile. Soft backgrounds (a sky, a plain wall) give the cleanest result.

2. Dark anti-hero edits

The opposite energy. Sharp shadows, a cold stare and a desaturated palette with one accent colour, usually red. Think of the brooding rival character, not the hero.

Get the look: start from a moody, side-lit photo with a neutral expression. Combining the Anime style with the Edgy vibe setting in PFPFORGE pushes it darker.

3. 90s retro cel

Muted, slightly grainy colour and the hand-painted feel of 90s TV anime. It's nostalgic and stands out precisely because it looks less digital than modern art.

Get the look: golden or late-afternoon light works well. Pair it with a simple outfit; retro cel styling suits jackets, school uniforms and big hair.

4. Shonen action hero

High energy and saturated: dramatic hair, intense eyes and speed-line backgrounds. Popular with gamers and streamers who want their avatar to feel like a main character.

Get the look: a confident expression and a slight upward camera angle make you look heroic.

5. Chibi and cute

Big head, tiny body, maximum cuteness. Chibi PFPs are great for wholesome, comedy and cosy-gaming creators. They also make great Discord emoji.

Get the look: an exaggerated expression (surprised, pouting, laughing) translates best into chibi proportions.

6. Anime meets another aesthetic

Some of the most eye-catching PFPs blend anime with a second aesthetic: anime + cyberpunk neon, anime + fairy core glow, anime + gothic shadows. Generate an anime version first, then compare it with the same photo in Cyberpunk or Gothic and see which reads as more "you".

Tips for the best anime result

  • Face the camera. Front-on or a slight three-quarter angle gives the cleanest anime features. Full profiles are harder to stylise.
  • Even lighting. Harsh shadows can turn into odd shapes once outlines are added.
  • Simple background. Busy rooms become visual noise. A plain wall or sky keeps the focus on you.
  • Expression is everything. Anime exaggerates emotion, so give it something to work with.

Is it OK to use an anime PFP?

Yes, especially one made from your own photo. Using an original, stylised version of yourself avoids the copyright and impersonation problems of using a real character's artwork, and it's more memorable too.
