FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?+
Yes. The free forge gives you unlimited in-browser styling across all 15 aesthetics. Pro ($4.99/month) adds real AI photo morphing and removes ads.
Do you store or sell my photos?+
No. In free mode your photo never leaves your device — styling happens in your browser. In Pro AI mode, the image is sent securely to the image service only to generate your result, and is never sold or used for marketing.
Where can I use my PFP?+
Anywhere — TikTok, Instagram, Discord, X, gaming profiles and more. You get a clean square PNG, the standard avatar shape everywhere.
What's the difference between free and Pro results?+
Free uses fast in-browser styling — color grading, tone and texture tuned per aesthetic. Pro uses a generative AI model that fully re-imagines your photo in the chosen style, so results are far more dramatic.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?+
Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription any time from the billing portal — no emails, no hoops.
