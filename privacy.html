Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

PFPFORGE turns your photos into stylised profile pictures. This policy explains what information we handle when you use the site, why, and the choices you have.

Your photos

  • Free mode (in-browser): the photo you upload is processed entirely on your own device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Pro AI mode: your photo and the style you choose are sent securely to our image-generation provider, fal.ai, only to create your result. The result is sent straight back to your browser. We do not store your photos, sell them or use them for marketing.

Pro subscriptions and payments

Payments are handled by Stripe, on Stripe's own checkout page, including card and PayPal payments. Your card or PayPal details never reach our servers. Stripe shares your email address and subscription status with us so we can unlock Pro. We store your email address, your Stripe customer ID and whether your subscription is active, and nothing else about your payment.

Login links

If you ask for a login link to restore Pro on another device, we send it to your email address using our email provider, Resend. The link expires after 15 minutes.

Cookies, local storage and advertising

  • Essential storage: we keep your Pro login token and your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so the site works.
  • Advertising: free visitors may see ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Your choice: when you first visit, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. Pro subscribers don't see ads.

Who we share data with

We only share data with the services needed to run PFPFORGE: Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), fal.ai (AI image generation, Pro only), Resend (login emails) and Google (advertising). We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your subscription record (email, Stripe customer ID and status) for as long as you have an account with us, and as required for accounting and tax purposes. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

PFPFORGE is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@YOURDOMAIN.com
