Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you forge.

Is it free to use?+
Yes. You can forge profile pictures for free. We keep the lights on with non-intrusive ads on the page.
Do you store or sell my photos?+
In the default in-browser mode, your photo never leaves your device — the styling happens right in your browser. If AI mode is enabled, your image is sent securely to the image service only to generate your result and is not sold or kept for marketing.
What can I use my PFP for?+
Anything — TikTok, Instagram, Discord, X, gaming profiles, and more. The output is a clean square PNG, the standard shape for avatars everywhere.
Why does the AI look different from the filter preview?+
The instant preview uses fast in-browser styling. When full AI mode is on, a generative model re-imagines your photo in the chosen aesthetic, so the result is more dramatic and creative.
Can I request a new aesthetic?+
Absolutely. New trending aesthetics are added regularly. If there's a style you want to see, reach out and it might land in the next update.
